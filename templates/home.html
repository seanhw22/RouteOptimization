Methodology

Greedy

A fast constructive heuristic that builds routes by inserting the nearest feasible customer at each step. Serves as a quick baseline solution with minimal computation time.

HGA — Hybrid Genetic Algorithm

Combines genetic algorithm operators (selection, crossover, mutation) with local search to balance solution quality and runtime. Effective for medium to large instances.

MILP — Mixed-Integer Linear Programming

A mathematical optimization model solved by Gurobi that guarantees optimal solutions. Best suited for small instances (up to 25 nodes) due to exponential complexity growth.

About

RouteForge is an undergraduate thesis project investigating the Multi-Depot Vehicle Routing Problem (MDVRP) applied to the distribution of consumer products — specifically packed food and beverages.

The problem scope includes:

  • Multiple depots serving a shared fleet of vehicles
  • Vehicle capacity and maximum operational hour constraints
  • Customer delivery time windows (deadline hours)
  • Item expiry constraints for perishable goods
  • Comparison of three algorithms: Greedy, HGA, and MILP
